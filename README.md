# 📌 Project Overview

**Project FORESIGHT** is an AI-powered Business Intelligence and Demand Forecasting platform developed using **Power BI** to help retail organizations optimize inventory planning, forecast demand, analyze customer behavior, and monitor business performance through interactive dashboards.

The platform transforms raw retail transaction data into meaningful business insights using advanced analytics, DAX calculations, and interactive visualizations, enabling data-driven decision-making across sales, inventory, and supply chain operations.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses often face challenges such as:

- Unpredictable customer demand
- Overstocking and stock-outs
- Lack of real-time business insights
- Inefficient inventory management
- Difficulty identifying high-value customers
- Seasonal demand fluctuations

Project FORESIGHT addresses these challenges by providing an AI-powered analytics platform that enables organizations to forecast demand, optimize inventory, and improve operational efficiency.
